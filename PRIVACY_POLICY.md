# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 7, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Talking Dog ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and handles information when you use the Talking Dog iOS application (the "App").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

During onboarding you create a profile for your dog. This includes:

- Your dog's name, breed, and age
- A photo of your dog (optional)
- Personality traits, voice style, and other descriptive information you choose to enter (bio, life story, favorite snacks, owner names)

This information is stored **locally on your device** using the device's secure storage. We do not maintain a user account system and do not require you to create an account.

### Information Generated During Use

- **Conversation history** — the messages exchanged between you and your dog's AI persona are stored locally on your device and retained across sessions so the dog can remember recent conversations.
- **Dog collar sensor data** — if you pair a Talking Dog collar, the App receives motion data (accelerometer and gyroscope readings) and behavior signals (e.g., tail wagging, barking) from the collar over Bluetooth. This data is processed on-device in real time to determine your dog's current state; it is not stored persistently.

### Information Sent to Our Backend

When you interact with the App, certain data is transmitted to our backend server to generate responses and audio:

- Your dog's profile (name, breed, personality traits, etc.)
- Recent conversation history (up to the last few messages)
- The trigger event that prompted a response (e.g., "tail wagging," "bark," or a button you tapped)

This information is sent solely to generate your dog's next spoken response and is not stored on our servers beyond what is needed to fulfill the request.

## 2. Third-Party Services

To generate your dog's responses and voice, our backend uses the following third-party AI services:

| Service | Purpose | Privacy Policy |
|---|---|---|
| **OpenAI** | Generates your dog's text responses | [openai.com/policies/privacy-policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy) |
| **Cloudflare AI (inworld/tts-2)** | Converts text responses to spoken audio | [cloudflare.com/privacypolicy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) |

Your dog's profile and conversation context are sent to OpenAI as part of each request. Please review OpenAI's privacy policy to understand how they handle API request data. We do not share your information with any other third parties for advertising or analytics purposes.

---

## 3. Device Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions:

- **Bluetooth** — to discover and connect to the Talking Dog collar
- **Microphone** — to record audio that is played back through the collar's speaker (audio is processed locally and sent over Bluetooth; it is not uploaded to any server)
- **Camera / Photo Library** — to let you set a photo of your dog; photos are stored locally on your device only
- **Local Network** — used during collar pairing

You can manage these permissions at any time in your device's Settings app.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

All dog profile information and conversation history are stored **locally on your device** using the device's operating system storage (`AsyncStorage`). We do not have a cloud database of user data.

When you delete the App, all locally stored data is removed from your device.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
